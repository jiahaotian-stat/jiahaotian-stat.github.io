## Research Projects

---

### Distributional Balancing Weights for Conformal Calibration under Covariate Shift

- **Advisor:** [Prof. Guanhua Chen](https://guanhuac.github.io/), University of Wisconsin–Madison
- **Period:** Mar. 2026 – Sep. 2026
- **Keywords:** Uncertainty Quantification · Distribution Shift · Conformal Prediction · Distributional Balancing

This project studies reliable uncertainty quantification under covariate shift through distributional balancing. The work develops reweighting methods for conformal calibration and investigates their connections to RKHS- and characteristic-function-based discrepancies.

### Heavy-Tailed Robustness of Multiple-Knockoff Aggregation Methods

- **Advisor:** [Prof. Lijun Wang](https://hohoweiya.xyz/), School of Mathematical Sciences, Zhejiang University
- **Period:** Sep. 2025 – Present
- **Keywords:** High-Dimensional Inference · Multiple Testing · Model-X Knockoffs · Heavy-Tailed Distributions

This project studies robust high-dimensional variable selection using multiple knockoffs, with a focus on false discovery control under heavy-tailed distributions and model misspecification.
